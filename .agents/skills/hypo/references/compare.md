# Compare with Canon

Compare the current formulation with the model's internal understanding of the
established scientific canon. Include two or three remembered academic
references when useful, but mark every reference and reference-dependent claim
as **remembered and unverified**. Do not browse or fabricate bibliographic
precision.

Return evidence capsules separating canonical agreement, canonical tension,
novel synthesis, and the claims most worth live verification. This operation
locates the formulation relative to established thinking; it does not establish
that the remembered canon is current or correct. End with the shared review
hook.
