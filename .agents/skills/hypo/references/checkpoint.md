# Checkpoint

Produce a compact standalone handoff that lets the scientific search resume
without replaying the conversation. Preserve the current question and search
coordinates, stable branch IDs and status, decisions and reasons, evidence
capsules, assumptions, contradictions, uncertainty, the leading formulation,
the strongest rival, and the next consequential choice or grounding action.

Compress repetition and discarded wording, not scientific dissent or negative
results. Distinguish user decisions from model proposals and evidence from
inference. Do not manufacture closure. Finish with the shared review hook.
