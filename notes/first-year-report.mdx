1. **Introduction**
   1. _Main research question and hypotheses_

      Under what conditions do gamma oscillations contribute causally to neural computation, and can it be applied in useful ways?
   2. _Research strategy: A) functions, B) mechanisms and C) applications_

      Starting reviews: [\[1\]](#ref-1); [\[2\]](#ref-2); [\[3\]](#ref-3).
2. **Background and literature review**

        1. Mention oscillations as a solution for various simple/core computational problems.

        1. _Efficiency, robustness and local selection_ — [\[21\]](#ref-21); [\[22\]](#ref-22); [\[23\]](#ref-23). Compare gamma and asynchronous computation with [\[24\]](#ref-24); assess the broader functional claim against [\[3\]](#ref-3).
        2. _Selective communication, attention and sensory routing_ — theory: [\[2\]](#ref-2); [\[25\]](#ref-25); [\[26\]](#ref-26); recordings: [\[27\]](#ref-27); [\[28\]](#ref-28). Constraints and alternatives: [\[29\]](#ref-29); [\[30\]](#ref-30); [\[31\]](#ref-31); [\[32\]](#ref-32).

        5. _Prediction-error signalling_ — framework: [\[40\]](#ref-40); experimental evidence: [\[41\]](#ref-41); [\[42\]](#ref-42). Alternative predictive functions and critical review: [\[43\]](#ref-43); [\[44\]](#ref-44).
        6. _Memory organisation, sequencing and routing_ — models and review: [\[45\]](#ref-45); [\[46\]](#ref-46); recordings: [\[47\]](#ref-47); [\[48\]](#ref-48). Interpret coupling cautiously using [\[49\]](#ref-49); [\[50\]](#ref-50); the recordings alone do not establish a necessary gamma mechanism.
        7. _Synaptic plasticity_ — [\[51\]](#ref-51); [\[52\]](#ref-52). Compare with spike-timing-dependent plasticity: [\[53\]](#ref-53). Separate phase sensitivity and gamma-associated plasticity from evidence that gamma is required for learning.

        Include criticisms and counterarguments for each proposed role.

     4. _Causal evidence and outstanding gaps_

        1. Gamma could be epiphenomenon, evolutionary history not solid.
        2. Mention limitations of current experimental mechanisms for larger network based phenomena.
        3. _Cell-type and timing interventions_ — [\[36\]](#ref-36); [\[22\]](#ref-22); [\[54\]](#ref-54); [\[55\]](#ref-55). Assess what each intervention isolates; changing inhibition can also change firing rates and excitability.
        4. _Coherence as cause or consequence_ — [\[31\]](#ref-31); [\[56\]](#ref-56); [\[57\]](#ref-57). Connectivity, power and frequency shifts provide competing explanations that must be tested.
        5. _Inference and measurement limits_ — [\[50\]](#ref-50); [\[49\]](#ref-49); [\[9\]](#ref-9). The references above do not establish an evolutionary explanation for gamma.
