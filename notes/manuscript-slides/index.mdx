<Slide title="Spike perturbations in trained classifiers." density="compact" layout="figure">
<div class="slide-figure manuscript-figure">

<PublishedFigure experiment="exp037" file="perturbation_curves.svg" id="loc-14" alt="Loop-off and loop-on test accuracy under spike deletion and baseline-relative spike insertion." >
  Figure 7: **Spike perturbations in trained classifiers.** **(A–B)** Inference-time perturbations of validation-selected unpenalised TR02 loop-off (red squares) and loop-on (black diamonds), without retraining, using the common post-training evaluation protocol. Lines and shading show mean ± sample SD across three independent training replicates; dashed lines mark 10% chance accuracy. **(A)** Independent deletion of transmitted E/I spikes, preserving neuronal resets. **(B)** Independent insertion into both populations at 0–200% of each network’s unperturbed E firing rate; aggregation matches relative doses, not hertz. The same nominal per-neuron insertion rate applies to E and I. Collisions add no event; inserted events cause no reset or refractory period. Both interventions affect readout and recurrent transmission. Exact dose grids and operations are given in Methods. Source experiment: [exp037](/exp037) — [*Dropped Spikes vs Added Noise.*](/exp037)
