## Parameters

The table reads the saved native mean-field model along the selected presentation's
compute ancestry. E/I pairs are ordered excitatory/inhibitory. Coupling is the
sum of effective conductance jumps over sources, averaged over target neurons,
including absent self-edges. All weights and time constants stay fixed during
Figure 1; only the three external rates change.

<Parameters />

The native noisy-LIF closure uses fixed resting driving forces and omits shunting,
input covariance and finite-population fluctuations. Rate-relaxation times are
phenomenological closure parameters, separate from membrane and synaptic decay
times. E times remain 24/16/20 ms for A/B/C; I times are calibrated to the closer
autonomous frequency targets using uncoupled constant inputs at the inherited hold
values, measured over 1500–2500 ms. These are finite-window estimates, not fixed
frequencies across the ramps. E→E and I→I sums remain at half their original values;
all weights and input schedules are inherited without firing-rate recalibration.
The autonomous control does not initialize Figure 1. Native trajectories retain
initial states, controls and solver settings; no spiking simulation or spiking
snapshot is claimed.
