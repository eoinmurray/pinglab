## Parameters

Values come from the native simulation-start snapshot and its recorded compute
configuration. Na / K pairs refer to sodium / potassium. The proposal's gate
voltages are filtered membrane voltages. Each step advances both gates from
the preceding voltage, evaluates their sigmoid conductances, then advances the
membrane voltage with those new conductances and the current interval's input.
