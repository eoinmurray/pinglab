## Abstract

Two single-seed shared-input conductance-LIF conditions differ only in scheduled
external excitation of inhibitory neurons. The saved AI-target and PING-target
conditions have E rates of {metric('AI', 'E_rate_hz')} and {metric('PING', 'E_rate_hz')} Hz and spectral peaks of {metric('AI', 'E_peak_hz')} and {metric('PING', 'E_peak_hz')} Hz,
respectively. These are historical scratch outputs: raw spikes, executed native
bundles and start snapshots were not retained. Metrics are transcribed from the
saved JSON, not independently recomputed; the condition names do not establish
an asynchronous-irregular state or a PING mechanism.

## Introduction

This comparison asks how the saved activity and descriptive state metrics differ
when external Input→I excitation is retained or removed. The source protocol uses
4,000 E neurons, 1,000 I neurons and a shared pool of 4,000 external sources, each
at 2.5 Hz, with the same seed (42) for both conditions.

<Table headers={['Metric','AI','PING']} rows={metricRows} number={1} label="Saved state metrics">
  Values are descriptive, from one seed over the half-open 50–1050 ms window.
  ISI CV is the standard deviation divided by the mean interspike interval,
  calculated for cells with at least 20 spikes, then summarised by the median.
  Qualifying-cell counts expose the limited AI coverage. Spectral peaks use
  0.5 ms population-count bins, a Hann FFT periodogram and a 5–150 Hz search;
  contrast is peak power divided by median power at 100–300 Hz. Maximum bin
  fraction is the largest 0.5 ms spike count divided by population size.
  Rhythmicity uses a pooled, uncentred autocorrelogram in 1 ms bins, overlap
  correction, three-bin smoothing and the first local trough after lag 1 ms;
  it is the difference between the preceding maximum and trough divided by
  their sum. Correlations pool the first 200 E and first 200 I cells, after
  binary occupancy in 1 ms bins, into the native 20 ms spike-count correlation
  calculation. SD describes finite upper-triangle pair correlations, not
  uncertainty over seeds. Numeric entries display two significant figures.
</Table>

## Discussion

The PING-target output has larger volleys and greater pooled correlation than
the AI target. AI E-cell CV is undefined because no E cell met the spike-count
threshold; AI I-cell CV describes only {metric('AI', 'I_cv_cells')} qualifying cells. A weakly correlated
pooled sample and low firing rate alone do not establish microscopic AI activity.
The missing raw events and native execution evidence limit this report to a
preserved historical comparison. The standalone code supports fresh native
execution, but those results have not been generated or substituted here.
