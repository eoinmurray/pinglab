## Methods

### 1. Couple the proposal's ionic neurons with graded inhibition

Each cell uses the [Task 1 ionic model](/exp146). For neuron index
$i\in\{1,2\}$ and channel $x\in\{\mathrm{Na},\mathrm{K}\}$,

$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}z_{x,i}}{\mathrm{d}t}
=\frac{V_i-z_{x,i}}{\tau_x},
\qquad g_{x,i}=\bar g_x\,\sigma\!\left(\gamma_x(z_{x,i}-b_x)\right),
\qquad \sigma(u)=\frac{1}{1+\exp(-u)}.
$$

Here $t$ is time in ms, $V_i$ is membrane voltage in mV, $z_{x,i}$ is filtered
voltage in mV, $\tau_x$ is the channel time constant in ms, $g_{x,i}$ and
$\bar g_x$ are channel and maximum conductance in µS, $\gamma_x$ is the sigmoid
slope in mV$^{-1}$, and $b_x$ is its midpoint in mV. The argument $u$ is
dimensionless.

For the graded synapse from neuron $j$ to neuron $i$, with $j\ne i$,

$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}z_{j\to i}}{\mathrm{d}t}
=\frac{V_j-z_{j\to i}}{\tau_s},
\qquad g_{j\to i}=\bar g_s\,\sigma\!\left(\gamma_s(z_{j\to i}-b_s)\right).
$$

Here $z_{j\to i}$ is the synaptic gate voltage in mV, $\tau_s$ its time
constant in ms, $g_{j\to i}$ and $\bar g_s$ the synaptic and maximum
conductance in µS, $\gamma_s$ the slope in mV$^{-1}$ and $b_s$ the midpoint in mV.
The membrane equation gains the inhibitory conductance term:

$$
C\frac{\mathrm{d}V_i}{\mathrm{d}t}
= I_i-g_L(V_i-V_L)
-g_{\mathrm{Na},i}(V_i-E_{\mathrm{Na}})
-g_{\mathrm{K},i}(V_i-E_{\mathrm{K}})
-g_{j\to i}(V_i-E_s).
$$

Here $C$ is capacitance in nF, $I_i$ injected current in nA, $g_L$ leak
conductance in µS, and $V_L$, $E_{\mathrm{Na}}$, $E_{\mathrm{K}}$ and $E_s$
are leak, sodium, potassium and inhibitory reversal potentials in mV.
These units preserve the proposal's numerical equations; its C/g/I units
require confirmation against the Toolbox.

At every step of length $\Delta t=0.001$ ms, all ionic and synaptic gates
advance by forward Euler using the preceding membrane voltages. New gate
values determine conductances; both membranes then advance using those new
conductances and the old voltages. The cells have no voltage reset.

### 2. Represent both synapses as native graph connections

SNNLab 0.24 lets a custom synapse declare `input_unit="mV"` and
`output_unit="uS"`. Each projection reads `pre.voltage` at the beginning of
the timestep and delivers the newly updated conductance to `post.inhibitory`.
Dimensionless projection weights are one; the physical maximum conductance
belongs inside the synapse, after the nonlinear gate. Zero added delay avoids
shifting the proposal's coupling by an extra timestep.

### 3. Run one HCO and validate its saved trajectory

Both membrane voltages and every gate start at −70 mV. Neuron 1 receives
1.0 nA for $5\leq t<10$ ms, then 0.8 nA; neuron 2 receives 0.8 nA for
$10\leq t<200$ ms. All other input intervals have zero current. One HCO runs
for 200 ms in float64 on CPU; no batch benchmark is performed.
Spikes are sampled upward −20 mV crossings, timed at the end of the crossing
timestep without interpolation. Strict alternation after 10 ms means every
consecutive event belongs to the other cell, with no simultaneous events.
