## A. Networks

<RunFigure report="exp171" experiment="exp171" file="network-circuit.svg" number="A1" id="fig-coba" alt="COBA with trainable sensory and readout matrices and frozen private inhibitory input." allowUnavailable={true}>
  Native COBA architecture. E and I denote excitatory and inhibitory neurons,
  X sensory input, R readout and U amplitude-scaled private events. Only X→E and
  E→R matrices train; E→I and I→E remain zero. Each U channel reaches its paired
  E neuron through a frozen 1-µS identity projection and 6-ms GABA filter with
  one-timestep delivery delay. Its event amplitude equals the per-image calibrated
  conductance increment divided by 1 µS. In the identity label, j and k index
  channels and recipients, and the Kronecker delta is one only when j equals k.
  W lists recipient then source; C identifies COBA. Labels describe loaded
  execution parameters, not compiler zero placeholders.
</RunFigure>

  its separately learned matrices; subscripts list recipient then source. For each
  fixed sensory encoding, PING inhibitory-conductance temporal moments determine
  the artificial generator. Teacher weights never train. COBA and PING checkpoints
  and configurations come exclusively from the same authenticated source run;
