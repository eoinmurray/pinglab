<RunFigure report="exp171" experiment="exp171" file="learning-curves.svg" number={1} id="fig-learning" alt="Training and validation loss, validation accuracy and excitatory firing across fine-tuning epochs." allowUnavailable={true}>
  Left: training and validation cross-entropy. Centre: validation accuracy. Right:
  mean E firing. Epoch zero is the inhibited original checkpoint; each training
  epoch contains ten updates; the final epoch follows the recorded configuration. Validation averages three fixed inhibitory draws
  for each of 100 images. Sensory encodings are fixed per image; training inhibitory
  events are redrawn each epoch from frozen fitted probabilities and amplitudes.
  Trials last 200 ms with no burn-in. The final epoch is the primary comparison;
  best-validation selection does not replace it. Curves show one seed without
  independent-replication uncertainty. A negative short pilot is inconclusive.

  Raster from the retained checkpoint with the lowest validation cross-entropy.
  The first validation image is shown without selection by activity or correctness,
  using the first of three fixed inhibitory draws. The full original validation
  batch is replayed to preserve numerical agreement with saved scores and rates.
  Red dots above: private artificial inhibitory events; black dots below: E spikes.
  The biological I population is disabled and silent. Each trial scores all 200 ms;
  only private conductance and pending events carry over from inhibitory
  prehistory (recorded duration in Appendix B); neurons and readout start fresh. The title gives the checkpoint
  epoch, true digit, prediction and E rate. One illustrative trial, not an
  independent test-set result.
