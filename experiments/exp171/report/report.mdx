## Methods

- **PING targets.** For each image, a frozen PING forward pass supplied each E
  neuron's inhibitory conductance. We measured its temporal mean and variance
  over the presentation:

  $$
  \mu_j=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}g^{\mathrm{PING}}_{j,n},
  \qquad
  v_j=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}
  \left(g^{\mathrm{PING}}_{j,n}-\mu_j\right)^2.
  $$

  Here, j indexes E neurons, n indexes timesteps, N is the number of timesteps,
  and g denotes inhibitory conductance. The student received the same sensory
  spike train.

- **Private Poisson input.** Each E neuron received independent Poisson counts,
  scaled by a fixed event amplitude and filtered through GABA:

  $$
  K_{j,n}\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda_j\Delta t),
  \qquad
  g_{j,n+1}=a g_{j,n}+q_jK_{j,n},
  \qquad
  a=\exp(-\Delta t/\tau).
  $$

  K is the event count, lambda the rate in Hz, q the conductance increment per
  event, a the decay factor, and delta t and tau the timestep and GABA decay
  constant in seconds. The update includes a one-timestep delay.

- **Moment fit.** Solving the discrete filter's stationary mean and variance
  equations gave the per-image, per-neuron parameters:

  $$
  q_j=\frac{(1+a)v_j}{\mu_j},
  \qquad
  \lambda_j=\frac{\mu_j^2(1-a)}{\Delta t(1+a)v_j}.
  $$

  Zero-conductance targets received no events. Fitted parameters remained fixed
  during fine-tuning; event draws changed each epoch. Biological I was silent.

- **Initialisation and test.** Inhibition-only prehistory initialised conductance;
  neurons started afresh at stimulus onset. Test-image fits used frozen PING
  passes without labels or weight updates. COBA/PING test scores were reused
  from TR02.

- **Scope.** Expected conductance moments were matched; spike timing, phase,
  shared correlations and feedback were not. Finite draws fluctuate around
  those moments.
