## Background

This experiment complements
[exp123](/exp123/) — [*Output Evidence Against Time and Gamma Cycles*](/exp123/).
We use its trained network and Figure 1 measurements, changing leak conductance
and membrane capacitance separately while holding inhibitory decay at 6 ms.
The checkpoint comes from
[exp022](/exp022/) — [*MNIST*](/exp022/).

## Results

### Leak conductance

<RunFigure report="exp134" experiment="exp134" allowUnavailable file="leak_overview.svg" number={1} id="fig-leak" alt="Four population-dynamics curves across leak multipliers and three illustrative excitatory and inhibitory spike rasters.">
  A–D show rhythm frequency, the percentage of excitatory neurons active per
  accepted cycle, total excitatory spikes per second, and rhythm contrast.
  Each setting uses the same 100 images, ten per digit, with ten paired random
  encodings per image. Leak conductance scales together in E and I populations;
  capacitance stays fixed. Frequency is the peak of the mean excitatory power
  spectrum. Contrast measures an autocorrelation lobe relative to its adjacent
  trough. Participation averages accepted cycles only. The dashed line marks
  the training setting, ×1. E–G show image 140 (digit 6), draw 42, at
  ×0.5, ×1 and ×2; excitatory spikes are black and inhibitory spikes red.
  All measurements cover 200 ms. No uncertainty estimates are shown.
</RunFigure>

We varied leak conductance in the excitatory ($E$) and inhibitory ($I$)
populations using the same dimensionless multiplier $m_j$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(g_{L,E},g_{L,I}) &= m_j\,(0.05,\,0.10)\;\mu\mathrm{S},\\
m_j &= 0.5\times 4^{j/18},\qquad j=0,\ldots,18.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$

Here $g_{L,E}$ and $g_{L,I}$ are the per-neuron leak conductances, and $j$
indexes the 19 logarithmically spaced settings from ×0.5 to ×2; $j=9$ gives
the shared ×1 baseline. E leak therefore ranges from 0.025 to 0.10 µS and I
leak from 0.05 to 0.20 µS. Membrane capacitances $C_{m,E}=1$ nF and
$C_{m,I}=0.5$ nF stay fixed. The passive membrane time constant
$\tau_{m,P}=C_{m,P}/g_{L,P}$, for population $P\in\{E,I\}$, is therefore
$\tau_{m,E}=20/m_j$ ms and $\tau_{m,I}=5/m_j$ ms. We held weights, GABA
decay (6 ms) and all other circuit settings fixed.

Accepted-cycle participation used {coverage('leak', 'accepted_draws')} of
1,000 trials per setting, representing {coverage('leak', 'participation_images')}
of 100 images.

### Membrane capacitance

  A–D show the same measurements as Figure 1 across membrane-capacitance
  multipliers. E and I capacitances scale together; leak conductances stay
  fixed. The image sample and every encoded spike train match Figure 1.
  The ×1 condition is the same shared baseline. E–G use the same illustrative
  image (140, digit 6) and draw 42 at ×0.5, ×1 and ×2, with excitatory spikes black and inhibitory
  spikes red. Participation includes accepted cycles only; no uncertainty
  estimates are shown.
</RunFigure>

We varied membrane capacitance in the excitatory ($E$) and inhibitory ($I$)
populations using the same dimensionless multiplier $m_j$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(C_{m,E},C_{m,I}) &= m_j\,(1.0,\,0.5)\;\mathrm{nF},\\
m_j &= 0.5\times 4^{j/18},\qquad j=0,\ldots,18.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
$$

Here $C_{m,E}$ and $C_{m,I}$ are the per-neuron membrane capacitances, and $j$
indexes the 19 logarithmically spaced settings from ×0.5 to ×2; $j=9$ gives
the shared ×1 baseline. E capacitance therefore ranges from 0.5 to 2 nF and I
capacitance from 0.25 to 1 nF. Leak conductances $g_{L,E}=0.05$ µS and
$g_{L,I}=0.10$ µS stay fixed. The passive membrane time constant
$\tau_{m,P}=C_{m,P}/g_{L,P}$, for population $P\in\{E,I\}$, is therefore
$\tau_{m,E}=20m_j$ ms and $\tau_{m,I}=5m_j$ ms. We held weights, GABA
decay (6 ms) and all other circuit settings fixed.

Accepted-cycle participation used {coverage('capacitance', 'accepted_draws')}
of 1,000 trials per setting, representing
{coverage('capacitance', 'participation_images')} of 100 images.
