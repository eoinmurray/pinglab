## Methods

Current Poisson protocol; figures follow their selected recorded method.

1. Measure PING's per-E conductance mean $\mu_j$ and variance $\sigma_j^2$ over
   the saved 200-ms trial. Index $j$ identifies an E neuron and its private input.
2. For timestep $\Delta t=0.1$ ms and GABA decay $\tau_G=6$ ms, define:

   <a id="eq-poisson-decay"></a>

   $$
   a=e^{-\Delta t/\tau_G}. \tag{1}
   $$

3. Fit the fixed event increment $q_j$ (µS):

   <a id="eq-poisson-amplitude"></a>

   $$
   q_j=(1+a)\frac{\sigma_j^2}{\mu_j}. \tag{2}
   $$

4. Fit the event rate $\lambda_j$ (Hz), expressing $\Delta t$ in seconds:

   <a id="eq-poisson-rate"></a>

   $$
   \lambda_j=\frac{\mu_j^2(1-a)}{\Delta t(1+a)\sigma_j^2}. \tag{3}
   $$

5. Draw independent event counts $C_{j,n}$ for each input and bin $n$:

   <a id="eq-poisson-counts"></a>

   $$
   C_{j,n}\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda_j\Delta t). \tag{4}
   $$

6. Deliver those counts through the one-step-delayed filter:

   <a id="eq-poisson-filter"></a>

   $$
   g_{j,n+1}=a g_{j,n}+q_jC_{j,n}. \tag{5}
   $$

   Here $g$ is inhibitory conductance (µS). The fitted parameters match its
   stationary expected mean and variance; sampled trials are not rescaled.
7. Generate 60 ms of inhibitory prehistory, then start neurons fresh. Keep all
   weights frozen. Report achieved conductance statistics and E firing for the
   scored 200 ms. Artificial rates differ from PING's biological I rates.
