### Shared production specification

All runs map Poisson-encoded pixels through 1,024 excitatory neurons to ten spiking output LIF neurons. COBA and PING use the same input-weight and readout-weight initialization distributions. COBA disables recurrent E/I coupling; PING adds a $1024 \to 256 \to 1024$ E/I feedback loop and uses stronger backward-pass gradient damping to stabilize training through that recurrent path.

These are specified production settings, not newly measured results. Unless a TR-specific sheet says otherwise, every condition–seed network uses this contract. In the tables, $\mathcal{N} ( \mu_{\text{init}} , \sigma_{\text{init}}^{2} )$ denotes a normal distribution with initialization mean $\mu_{\text{init}}$ and standard deviation $\sigma_{\text{init}}$; $\max ( 0 , x )$ replaces a sampled weight $x$ by zero when negative. LIF means leaky integrate-and-fire.
