## Abstract

We trained two-layer conductance-based networks to recognize spoken digits represented by cochlear spike events. Both COBA and PING learned under the calibrated initialization and optimization recipe, with PING achieving higher development-validation accuracy in the paired replicate. Matched inference rasters show how the trained layers respond to one held-out utterance. This single-seed comparison and illustrative response do not establish competitive test performance or a general benefit from inhibition.

1. **Calculate classification scores.** For each utterance, we scored class $c$ as

   $$
   z_c=G\,\bar u_c\,\frac{r_{\mathrm{ref}}}{\max(\bar r_{E2},r_{\mathrm{floor}})}. \tag{1}
   $$

   Here $z_c$ is the class score, $\bar u_c$ is the mean voltage of class $c$'s non-spiking readout over the full one-second presentation, and $\bar r_{E2}$ is the firing rate averaged over all 128 E2 neurons and that presentation. We used gain $G=20$, reference rate $r_{\mathrm{ref}}=20\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and denominator floor $r_{\mathrm{floor}}=1\,\mathrm{Hz}$. We predicted the class with the largest score. The common positive scaling leaves that decision unchanged for fixed weights, but affects cross-entropy and its training gradients.

2. **Initialize weights.** We used seed 42 and identical initial learned matrices for COBA and PING. Input→E1 and E1→E2 conductances were drawn independently from Gaussian parents with mean 0.09 μS and SD 0.009 μS, with negative draws clamped to zero. We independently zeroed each entry with probability 0.95 for Input→E1 and 0.90 for E1→E2, then divided by the source population size times the retained fraction: $700\times0.05=35$ and $128\times0.10=12.8$, respectively. The parent distribution therefore describes values before sparsification and normalization, not final per-edge conductances. Initial zeros were not permanent connectivity masks, and these matrices remained nonnegative during training. E2→readout weights were drawn from a signed Gaussian with mean zero and SD 0.1 μS, then centered by subtracting the mean across the 128 source neurons separately for each class; no fan-in scaling or initial sparsification was applied. In each PING layer, fixed uniform E→I and I→E weights were $0.05/128=0.000390625\,\mu\mathrm{S}$ and $0.05/32=0.0015625\,\mu\mathrm{S}$ per edge, respectively, giving 0.05 μS summed incoming conductance per target in either direction. COBA had no inhibitory feedback connections.
