## Abstract

{data ? <p>Can a uniform rate cue select which of two spatial patterns a receiving spiking circuit represents? A and B receive different Gaussian sensory patterns and project through identical spatial weight matrices to C. Extra drive switches from A to B at {cfg.switch_ms} ms. At the selected cue of {data.selection.cue_hz} Hz/channel, the attended Gaussian fractions are {round(epochs[0].er, 3)} and {round(epochs[1].er, 3)}; direct source-to-C profile scores are {round(epochs[0].fidelity.copy_r_squared, 3)} and {round(epochs[1].fidelity.copy_r_squared, 3)}. Brief competing-pattern excursions remain. This tuned single-seed demonstration uses a cue and feedback setting selected on the displayed seed; it does not establish generalisation or isolate a causal communication-through-coherence mechanism.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}
