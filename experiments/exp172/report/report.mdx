## Abstract

{data ? <p>Can a uniform rate cue select which of two spatial patterns a receiving spiking circuit represents? A and B receive different Gaussian sensory patterns and project through identical spatial weight matrices to C. Extra drive switches from A to B at {cfg.switch_ms} ms. At the selected cue of {data.selection.cue_hz} Hz/channel, the attended Gaussian fractions are {round(epochs[0].er, 3)} and {round(epochs[1].er, 3)}; direct source-to-C profile scores are {round(epochs[0].fidelity.copy_r_squared, 3)} and {round(epochs[1].fidelity.copy_r_squared, 3)}. Brief competing-pattern excursions remain. This tuned single-seed demonstration uses cue, inhibition and coupling settings selected on the displayed seed; it does not establish generalisation or isolate a causal communication-through-coherence mechanism.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}

- <span id="eq-switching-success" /> **Switching success:** $S_e=N_{\mathrm{correct},e}/N_e$, where $e$ identifies the attend-A or attend-B period, $N_e$ counts its retained complete C PING cycles, and $N_{\mathrm{correct},e}$ counts cycles dominated by its attended pattern. Unresolved cycles count as failures; 1 means perfect selection.
- <span id="eq-cycle-flickering" /> **Flickering:** $F=N_{\mathrm{changes}}/N_{\mathrm{pairs}}$, where $N_{\mathrm{changes}}$ counts dominant-pattern changes across $N_{\mathrm{pairs}}$ adjacent, resolved PING-cycle pairs within the specified cue period. Unresolved cycles break adjacency. Zero means stable selection; 1 means alternation every cycle. With no eligible pairs, F is unavailable.
- <span id="eq-switching-latency" /> **Switching latency:** $L_e=\min\{k\ge1:z_{e,k}=z_{e,k+1}=z_{e,k+2}=a_e\}$, where $z_{e,k}$ is the dominant pattern in C PING cycle $k$ of period $e$, and $a_e$ its attended pattern. Cycle 1 is the first complete trough-to-trough cycle starting at or after that period’s cue onset; the straddling cycle is excluded. Report the first cycle of the three-cycle run, not its confirmation cycle. Unresolved cycles break the run; no qualifying run means “Not observed”.
