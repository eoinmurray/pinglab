## Abstract

{data ? <p>A frozen TR02 (no rate penalty) PING network was evaluated at {cfg.pool_sizes.join(', ')} inhibitory neurons, including its original {cfg.original_n_i}-neuron pool. Excitatory population size and trained sensory/readout weights were fixed. I→E weights were multiplied by the original-to-retained pool-size ratio; E→I strengths were unchanged. For one identical digit-zero presentation, E firing rates ranged from {round(Math.min(...data.rates.map(row => row.E.mean_hz)), 2)} to {round(Math.max(...data.rates.map(row => row.E.mean_hz)), 2)} Hz. This is one trained network and one nested inhibitory-subset realization; the comparison measures compensated pool reduction.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}
