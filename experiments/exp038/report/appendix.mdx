export const nativeValue = value => {
  if (value === undefined || value === null) return 'Unavailable';
  if (Array.isArray(value)) return value.map(nativeValue).join(' / ');
  if (typeof value === 'object') return value.value === undefined ? JSON.stringify(value) : `${nativeValue(value.value)} ${value.unit ?? ''}`.trim();
  return String(value);
};

## Appendix: Training and probe settings

Training and probe settings are listed in [Parameters](#parameters).

The broader activity frontier contains 36 classifiers: two network configurations, three seeds, and six activity conditions (penalty off or ceilings of 25, 10, 5, 2.5 and 1 Hz). Its summaries preserve selected and final-epoch validation accuracy, final-epoch training E rate, and across-seed means and SEM; these are distinct from the inference measurements above. The loop-transfer comparison used only the three unpenalised feedforward classifiers. The [exp025](/exp025) — [*Accuracy and Firing Rate With and Without Inhibition*](/exp025) describes the broader training design.

Feedforward and loop-enabled training used voltage-gradient damping of 1 and 1,000 respectively; this difference affects the auxiliary between-model comparisons, not the within-classifier inference intervention. Dale’s law was enforced, adaptive thresholds were disabled, and membrane time constants were not trained.

Illustrative snapshots use test-image index 0, not selection by digit class. A fixed pseudorandom sample selects 200 E and 64 I neurons for display; reported firing rates use the full populations. Uniform-input E+I overlays add the two population means without weighting by neuron count, and are not a whole-network mean. Neither the transfer rasters nor these firing curves provide a spectral gamma-frequency estimate. The accuracy decline is consistent with changing the trained network’s dynamics, but does not identify the readout as its sole cause or test recovery by retraining.

## Parameters

Model values are read from original native snapshots through the pinned
presentation's compute ancestry. Paired values are E / I. Initializer parent means and SDs describe the original
weight distributions, in µS for conductance projections and dimensionless for
the current-based readout; they are not measured trained-weight statistics. Missing snapshots
or fields are unavailable; current recipes and historical copied tables are
not substituted. The recorded configuration identifies the input, numerical
and analysis choices of that computation; it does not establish a separate
upstream training protocol.
