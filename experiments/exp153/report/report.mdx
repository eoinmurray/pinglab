## Methods

1. **Define.** The standalone recipe initializes its own fourteen-pathway
   neuronal reference and reduces incoming weight sums and explicit delays to
   four population rates and fourteen conductance filters. Gain settings are
   recorded with each native model. Local recurrence and rate relaxation were
   selected to produce sustained oscillatory rates; cross-circuit timing was
   explored while checking both the onset display and coupling map. This is
   qualitative parameter selection, not an independent validation against exp133.

2. **Integrate.** Use DOP853 with constant prehistory, initial rates of 1 Hz and
   zero conductances. [Table A1](#parameters) gives the grid, timing and numerical
   settings; the native exports retain the complete configuration.

3. **Measure.** For a full map, derive phase from E-rate peaks and calculate PLV, drift and span,
   requiring sustained rhythms and valid uncoupled calibration. Extract the onset
   display from the selected map trajectory, or from a separately recorded onset-only
   calculation when only Figure 2 is refreshed.
