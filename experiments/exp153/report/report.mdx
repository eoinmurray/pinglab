## Abstract

Reciprocal coupling arrests phase drift between two delayed mean-field circuits
derived from [exp133](/exp133). Circuit B's rate relaxation is 20% faster,
creating a visible intrinsic mismatch.

## Results

Relative phase falls by 1.7 cycles before coupling at 500 ms, then settles near
−2 cycles.

## Methods

SNNLab reduces the imported network to four rates and ten conductance filters,
with 0.1 ms internal and 2 ms cross-circuit delays.

<MeanFieldModel />

Both trajectories use constant prehistory, initial rates of 1 Hz and zero
conductances. Integration uses DOP853 with maximum step 0.1 ms and tolerances
$10^{-8}$ relative/$10^{-10}$ absolute. Phase statistics require sustained regular
rhythms; locking thresholds and all parameters are in the appendix. The unfitted
closure omits input covariance, finite-population fluctuations and shunting.
