## Abstract

We compared spiking E/I networks with EE/II recurrence enabled or absent. Both used the same timestep, duration, seed, populations, and neuron parameters. In each case, changing three external-drive settings produced a low-correlation AI-like state and a strongly rhythmic PING state. Thus both regimes can appear with or without EE/II recurrence.
