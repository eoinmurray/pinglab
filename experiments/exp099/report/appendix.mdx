## Network diagrams

The circuit contains 1,600 excitatory and 400 inhibitory neurons with recurrent AMPA and GABA synapses ([Fig. A1](#loc-5)). Each neuron receives independent excitatory Poisson input; recurrent I neurons provide inhibition.

  Figure A1: Structural view of the compiled network, showing the E and I populations, recurrent AMPA and GABA projections, and private excitatory inputs. Input dimensions describe aggregate channels, one per target neuron; both private inputs provide excitation. $X_E$ and $X_I$ denote private excitatory input banks targeting E and I. Every projection is labelled $W_{XY}$, with target $X$ first and source $Y$ second. Arrowheads show excitation; bars show inhibition. Recording aliases and score reductions are omitted.
