## Network diagrams

The circuit contains 1,600 excitatory and 400 inhibitory neurons with recurrent AMPA and GABA synapses ([Fig. A1](#loc-5)). Each neuron receives independent excitatory Poisson input; recurrent I neurons provide inhibition.
