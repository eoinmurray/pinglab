## Abstract

{data ? <p>Can an extra input change which of two sending circuits a receiving circuit follows? We simulated two senders, A and B, connected equally to receiver C. An extra input switched from A to B at {cfg.switch_ms} ms, while their sensory inputs and connections stayed fixed. C's rhythm followed the cued sender more closely: timing consistency on a scale from 0 to 1 was {round(epochs[0].senders.A.plv, 3)} before the switch and {round(epochs[1].senders.B.plv, 3)} afterward. This single tuned simulation shows a switch in rhythmic alignment; it does not show better information transfer or reliability across repeated trials.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}
