## Abstract

{data ? <p>Switching a constant-rate cue from A to B reversed C's spatial rate contrast from {round(epochs[0].spatial.C_contrast, 3)} to {round(epochs[1].spatial.C_contrast, 3)}. Both senders retained fixed Gaussian inputs and equal topographic projections to C. This demonstrates spatial selection in one tuned realization; timing and rate effects remain confounded.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}
