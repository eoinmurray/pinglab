These are canonical parameters for decent conductance-based (COBA) and
pyramidal–interneuron gamma (PING) behaviour, following the shared TR01/TR02
recipe in [exp022](/exp022). E and I denote excitatory and inhibitory neurons;
paired values below are E / I. LIF means leaky integrate-and-fire.
